Issues fixed

1. Mac setup initially required scrolling past every TV camera and weather field.
   Those fields now live in a collapsed, clearly named TV display settings section.
   The legacy flat approval payload remains accepted.
2. Validation errors reset role/name/code. HA's current schema markers and suggested
   values now preserve those fields and nested TV selections on retry.

The initial frontend loading problem was missing voice-related dependencies in
the minimal local test environment. Installing the official required packages
resolved get_services and frontend startup; no BowPane authorization was relaxed.
